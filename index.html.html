Water (Permits) Regulations, 2001 — S.I 206 of 2001

Groundwater Abstraction Permits

1. What is a Permit?

A legal document issued by the Council under the Water Act that grants the right to extract groundwater for specified non-primary purposes.

It legally authorizes pumping, sets maximum allowable volumes, and mandates compliance like water meters and usage returns.

2. Who Should Apply?

  • Commercial & Industrial: Factories, hotels, lodges, and beverage producers.
  • Agricultural: Farms abstracting for irrigation or livestock.
  • Institutions: Schools, hospitals, and housing estates.
  • Primary Users: Those exceeding 5,000 cubic meters.
  • New Drillers: Anyone sinking a new borehole (rural or urban).

3. Why Should You Apply?

  • Legal Compliance: Avoid prosecution, fines, or borehole sealing orders.
  • Protection: Legally safeguards your water rights against encroachers.
  • Sustainability: Helps prevent over-abstraction and depletion.
  • Investment Security: Enhances property valuation and verifies compliance for audits.

Borehole Application Process

Applicants are required to visit our Tsholotsho or Plumtree offices with all compulsory documentation for submission and verification.

Rural Setup Requirements

  • Letter from the Village Head (Incwadi ka Sabhuku)
  • Surveyor's Report
  • National Identity Card (ID Card)
  • Homestead Tax Card (Ikhadi lomuzi)
Submit physical originals and photocopies at Plumtree or Tsholotsho offices.

Urban Setup Requirements

  • Proof of Residence or Proof of Ownership of the land/house
  • Surveyor's Report
  • National Identity Card (ID Card)
Verification is conducted by Council hydro-geologists prior to permit issuance.
